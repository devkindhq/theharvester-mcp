# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email security@devkind.com.au with details and reproduction steps. Do not open a public issue.

We aim to respond within 5 business days. Please avoid automated scanning of third-party
infrastructure (our Docker Hub images delegate to public OSINT sources; test against your own
domains).

## Scope

- This repository's code (server.py, Dockerfile, CI).
- Out of scope: vulnerabilities in upstream theHarvester (report to its maintainers) and in
  source APIs (report to those providers).
